### Inferences
- The corpus confirms substantial review and administration exposure. Unequal task sizes, incomplete old PageSpace histories, native/import duplication and dissimilar windows prohibit attributing the total token ratio to workflow efficiency.
- Classifier counters are overlapping occurrence proxies, not disjoint percentages of effort. Shell-level command inspection is required before calling a regex hit a true CI rerun or retry.

### Gaps
- No billing export or common provider pricing basis was audited. No token-to-dollar estimate is justified.
- No full cross-session parent lineage deduplication beyond imports and initial token baseline; no proof the machine history contains every historical conversation from other computers.
- Exact CPU time and human-active time cannot be reconstructed. Session spans include idle periods. Bounded interevent durations in inventory are exploratory proxies, not agent-work estimates.

### Takeaway
Independent review repeatedly finds material defects that clean tests miss. PageSpace offers examples of broad reuse and fast implementation, but its own record also shows overplanning, long-lived integration and missed tests: the balance should retain focused functional review while removing redundant full-gate repetition and prerequisite ceremony.

### Inferences
- Reserve full service/CI acceptance for the composed merge candidate; independently finish code review with clear evidence limits as soon as functional findings are assessed.
- Use broad outcome slices and existing components where boundaries are understood. Track producer dependencies as provisional integration obligations, not universal barriers to implementing consumers on branches.
- Target high-value review at auth, concurrency, persistence, native-form/hydration, mobile layout and real journeys; reuse fixed candidate evidence for unchanged paths, delta-review fixes, and avoid rebuilding/red-testing unrelated suites at every artifact revision.
- Cap repetitive plan revision by asking whether the next revision changes product behavior or only administrative dependency spelling. The corpus contains both legitimate owner revisions and repetitive procedural corrections; they must not be conflated.

### Gaps
- Repository/PR analysis is owned by the other researcher; conversation claims above should be cross-checked with PR diffs/timelines before treating all agent self-reports as verified delivery.
- No matched same-size completed PageSpace and Daisy feature pair supports a numeric speed factor; examples identify mechanisms rather than estimate causal effect size.
